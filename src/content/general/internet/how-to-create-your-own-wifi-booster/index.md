One of the most frequent question asked when it comes to WiFi is if we can somehow extend the range of our router. You can purchase a repeater/ WiFi booster from the market to do the job if you wish to. But it is so much fun to do it yourself.

For those people who are unaware of a WiFi booster, it extends the range of your WiFi router to give you a relatively better signal strength.

### REQUIREMENTS

The following materials are required:

1. A Cardboard (No special kind but it must be easily foldable)
2. A Pair of scissors
3. Wrapping foil (Aluminum is suggested)
4. Any adhesive
5. Thread

[![wifi booster materials required](./img-20150530-100712-e1432999978801-300x151.jpg)](./img-20150530-100712-e1432999978801-300x151.jpg)

### STEPS

1. Cut the cardboard into a square shape.
2. Paste the foil over the card board in such a manner that the cardboard is completely covered with foil on one side.[![wifi range extender](./img-20150530-101534-300x225.jpg)](./img-20150530-101534-300x225.jpg)
3. Wait for glue to dry, then make a whole exactly at the center of the card board.[![wifi range extender](./img-20150530-102049-225x300.jpg)](./img-20150530-102049-225x300.jpg)
4. now just fold the cardboard into an satellite like state and tie it with the thread using both the ends.![wifi range extender](./img-20150530-101754-225x300.jpg)![wifi range extender](./img-20150530-102538-225x300.jpg)
5. Place this folded cardboard over the antenna of your WIFI router.[![wifi booster diy setup](./img-20150530-102647-300x225.jpg)](./img-20150530-102647-300x225.jpg)

This is the cheapest method out there but it is one that works! I have been personally using this for the past 6 months. It gives about a 10-12% boost to your WiFi strength. When I checked for the signal strength with and without the DIY method, here are the results, before applying:
